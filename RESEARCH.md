# Research notes for the owner

These notes use only the supplied source files and are not legal advice.

1. **How does the directive define statutory interest for late payment?**

   It is simple interest at a rate equal to the reference rate plus at least eight percentage points. **Citation:** Source 1, Article 2(6).

2. **The owner's note says the EU maximum late fee is 25 EUR. Is that right?**

   No. The extract says the creditor is entitled to a minimum fixed sum of EUR 40 as compensation for recovery costs when late-payment interest becomes payable. The extract does not state an EU maximum late fee of EUR 25. **Citation:** Source 1, Article 6(1)–(3); Source 2, item 1.

3. **Does the business have to send a reminder before it is entitled to interest and to the fixed compensation sum?**

   No. For business-to-business transactions, the creditor is entitled to late-payment interest without a reminder when it has met its obligations and has not received timely payment (unless the debtor is not responsible for the delay). The fixed sum is payable without a reminder when interest becomes payable. **Citation:** Source 1, Article 3(1) and Article 6(1)–(2).

4. **What is the current ECB reference rate?**

   Not in the sources. The extract defines a reference rate and says which dates determine its use, but it gives no current rate. An up-to-date ECB source would be needed. **Citation:** Source 1, Article 2(7)(a) and Article 3(2).
